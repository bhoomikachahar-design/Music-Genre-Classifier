System Architecture

End-to-End Audio Signal to Prediction Pipeline

As documented in the project synopsis, this system integrates digital signal processing (DSP) and supervised machine learning to provide transparent, interpretable audio classification.

1

Audio Ingestion

Decodes raw audio bytes, resamples to 22,050 Hz, converts multi-channel to mono, and applies amplitude peak normalization.

y(t), fs = 22,050 Hz
2

Time-Frequency (STFT)

Divides audio into overlapping frames and applies Fast Fourier Transform to extract spectral energy distribution across frequencies.

X(m, ω) = STFT{y(t)}
3

MFCC & Timbral Extraction

Applies Mel-filterbanks and Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) for 20 MFCCs, plus Chroma, Spectral Centroid, Rolloff, HPSS & Tempo.

MFCC 1..20, Centroid, ZCR
4

Feature Aggregation

Computes temporal mean and variance across all acoustic frames, assembling the calibrated 58-dimensional tabular feature vector.

x ∈ ℝ⁵⁸ vector
5

Supervised Inference

The feature vector is evaluated by trained Random Forest / SVM classifiers to compute posterior probabilities across all 10 genres.

P(Genre | x) → Top-1
